About

01 designer

I started as a software engineer, and it still shows in how I design: I think in states, constraints, and grids before I think in mood boards. My UI/UX work leans minimalist or brutalist — never decorative for its own sake — because I want every choice to be one I can explain, and one I can build.

Right now I split my time between UI systems and gameplay mechanics for a small studio, and personal projects that push my design range further: noir web experiences, editorial layouts, and interfaces reconstructed pixel-for-pixel from Figma into working code.
